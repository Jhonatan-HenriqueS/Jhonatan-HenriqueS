## About

I met programming at 14, through a cousin who works in the field. In 2024 I started studying on my own, with YouTube, AI, dedication and effort.

Today I build complete web applications: interface, business rules and database. I like marketing, sales and communication, and that shapes how I design a system: I start from the real pain of the person who will use it.

I believe everything is possible with faith in God and dedication.

## How I work

I use AI as an assistant, not a substitute. Logic, architecture and requirements are mine: I research references and real pain points, define the goal and the audience, and record everything in an MVP report before implementing. Then I build in stages, each with clear context, scope and objective.

AI tools I use: Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex and Gemini.
